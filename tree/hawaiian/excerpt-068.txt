Ua kanalua kou Komite i ka liiki i keia manawa ke hoala ia ae ka ninau a hana paha e pili ana i na mea hope elua i hoike ia ae nei, no ka mea, ua hooia ia kela mau mea e ka Luna Nui o na Hana Hou o ke Aupuni a ua pau.

Aka, ke manao nei kou Komite a pela lakou e hoike aku nei imua o keia Hale, he mea pono o kela mau dala i uku ia ia Messrs Lucas a me Thonias i oi ae mamua o ka mea pono e.hoihoi ia mai i ke Aupuni, a ina he mea e pono ai ia hoihoi ana mai e hoomaka ka Loio Kuhina e hana aku i na hana e hoihoi ia mai ai.

Aole i manao kou Komite he mea waiwai ka helu papa ana aku i na olelo e pili ana i keia hana i hoike mua ia ae nei a me na hana kue kanawai i hana ia e pili ana i keia mau mea, aka, ua manao lakou ua lawa paha na hana i hoike ia ae nei, ina e hoomau ia ana, i mea e hoolilo ai i ke kanawai i mea ole, a o na hoike hoi o na Keena Aupuni i mea hihia a huikau launa ole, a he mea pono e hoike ia aku na hoahewa kaumaha loa ana maluna o ia mau hana.

HoNOLULu Oahu, lune 4, 1888. I KA MEA Hanohano, Alex. Young, Lunahoomolu o ke KomUe i koho ia e nana i na hoolilo e pili ana i ka Hale Hookolokolo Hoomalu Hou o Honolulu,

Aloha oe: — E hke me kau kauoha ua loaa ia maua na mea nona na inoa malalo iho e hoike aku e like me keia mahope ae nei e pili ana i ka Hale Hookolokolo Hoo- malu.

E olaolu oe e ike iho ua hookomo puia xiae keia hoike keia mau palapala mahope ae nei :

1. Ka Hoike e hoike ana i na haawina ame nahoolilo ana nona ke koena he $15,700 i hoailonaia *' A."
