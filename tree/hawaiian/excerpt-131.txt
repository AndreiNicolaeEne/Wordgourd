Aka hoi, ina no e hoopili ia ae keia mau rula naauao keia au, eia no nae no ka nui loa no o na mea e hiki ai ke noonoo ia iloko o keia kumuhana, nolaila, e kakai- kahi wale ana no na mea e hiki ke hooholo ia me ke kina^una'u ole, aia wale no, a ua noonoo loihi ia e na kanaka i kamaaina loa me na lawelawe ia ana o keia mau ano hana, a e hiki ai hoi ia lakou ke waiho mai i mau hoololi e kupono ai no ua wahi 'la.

No keia mau kumu, a mamuli pu hoi o na kahua o ka pono, ke koi aku nei makou e koho ia ona Papa Luna Nana Hale Paahao, a i ole, i poe luna hooponopono paha nana e hooponopono na hoomalu ana o ka hale paahao.

na mokiiaiiia o Amerika Huipuia, aole pu no hoi keia he inanao hou i Hawaii nei; no ka mea, ma na kanawai 1851, ua hoakaka ia ke koho ana i na Luna Nana Hale Paahao, ma kela a me keia mokupuni o Maui, Ha- waii, Oahu a me Kauai, a no kekahi mau makaliiki loihi mahope mai ua koho ia no keia poe Luna Nana; a ma ka makaiiiki 1884, mamuli o ke noi ana a ke Komite i koho ia ai e hoike mai e pili ana i na makai a me na hale paahao, ua waiho ia aku kekahi bila kanawai e hoomaopopo ana no ke koho ana aku i Papa Luna Nana, a ua hooholo ia keia bihi kanawai e ka Ahaolelo, koe wale no ko ka Moi apono ole ana.

Aole no i nianao kou Komite he kupono ke hoala ia he kumu hoopaapaa ma keia mea i hoike ia ae la ma keia hoike, a aole pu no hoi lakou i manao e hoike aku i na pomaikai a pau e ulu mai ana mailoko mai o keia mea; aka, ke wailio aku nei nae lakou i kekahi bila kanaw^ai e hoakaka ana no ke koho ana aku i kekahi papa ia ano.
