Formal Investigation 1 Where— a the person making the representation has—

i made a request in accordance with regulation 16(4)(c); or

ii refused an offer of discussion under regulation 17(2); or

b following a discussion under regulation 17(2) the representation has not been resolved to the satisfaction of the person making it;

1 the local authority must consider the representation in accordance with this regulation.
