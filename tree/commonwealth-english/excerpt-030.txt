Statutory Instruments
Infrastructure Planning

The Northampton Gateway Rail Freight Interchange (Correction) Order 2020
Made6th October 2020
Coming into force7th October 2020

The Northampton Gateway Rail Freight Interchange Order 20191 (“the Northampton Gateway RFI Order”) contains correctable errors as defined in paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 4 to the Planning Act 20082 (“the Act”).
Before the end of the relevant period referred to in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a request in writing to correct errors in the Northampton Gateway RFI Order and, in accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed each local planning authority for the area in which the land to which the Northampton Gateway RFI Order relates is situated that the request has been received.
The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order—

PART 1PRELIMINARY
Citation and Commencement
This Order may be cited as the Northampton Gateway Rail Freight Interchange (Correction) Order 2020 and comes into force on 7th October 2020.

The Northampton Gateway RFI Order is amended as follows—

In article 23(1) (guarantees in respect of payment of compensation), for “33” substitute “32”.

In article 34(3)(b) (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development), after “in relation to that”, for “and” substitute “land”.
