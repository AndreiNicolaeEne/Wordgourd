Rating And Valuation, England The Non-Domestic Rating (Collection and Enforcement) (Local Lists) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2011

Made Laid before Parliament Coming into force

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 143(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 9 to, the Local Government Finance Act 1988 , makes the following Regulations:

Application, citation and commencement 1 These Regulations, which apply to England only, may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating (Collection and Enforcement) (Local Lists) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2011 and shall come into force on 31st March 2011.
