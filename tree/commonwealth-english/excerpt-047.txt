Statutory Instruments
Exiting The European Union, England

Food, England The Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020
Made11.10 p.m. on 31st December 2020
Laid before Parliament4th January 2021
Coming into force1st January 2021

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 11A(1) of Schedule 2 to the Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations 2011 M1, makes the following Regulations.

Annotations:
Marginal Citations
M1S.I. 2011/1197. These Regulations are amended by S.I. 2020/1361. Paragraph 11A of Schedule 2 is inserted by regulation 2(2) of the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 (S.I. 2020/1481). There are other amending instruments but none are relevant.

Citation, application and commencement1.
These Regulations may be cited as the Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

They come into force on the day after the day on which IP completion day falls, immediately following the coming into force of regulation 40 of the Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 M2 and the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 M3.

These Regulations apply in relation to England only.
