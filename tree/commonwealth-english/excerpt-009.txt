Environmental Protection Licensing (marine)

Marine Pollution The Marine Licensing (Application Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

Made Laid before Parliament Coming into force

In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 67(2) and (3) and 316(1)(b) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 , the Secretary of State, as the appropriate licensing authority under section 113(2)(a), (4)(a), (6)(a) and (8) of that Act , makes the following Regulations.

Citation and commencement 1 These Regulations may be cited as the Marine Licensing (Application Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 1st May 2014.

Amendment of the Marine Licensing (Application Fees) Regulations 2014

2 Regulation 6 (deposits in cases where fee payable at hourly rate) of the Marine Licensing (Application Fees) Regulations 2014 is amended as follows—

a in paragraph (1), for “licensee” substitute “applicant”; and

b in paragraph (4), for “application”, where it first occurs, substitute “applicant”.
