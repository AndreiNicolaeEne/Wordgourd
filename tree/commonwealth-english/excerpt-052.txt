Highways, England The A66 Trunk Road (Scotch Corner Junction to Violet Grange Farm) Order 2014

Made Coming into force The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980 and now vested in him and :

1 This Order may be cited as The A66 Trunk Road (Scotch Corner Junction to Violet Grange Farm) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 15th September 2014.

2 In this Order— 1 all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway; and

2 — a “the main new road” means the new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this Order;

b “the new trunk roads” means the main new road and the new roundabout;

c “the plan” means the plan numbered HA 10/MP/115 marked “The A66 Trunk Road (Scotch Corner Junction to Violet Grange Farm) Order 2014”, comprising a plan folio cover, a location plan and a site plan, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at Document Deposit Service, Communities and Local Government, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA; and

3 The main new road and the new roundabout shall become trunk roads from the date when this Order comes into force.

4 The centre line of each of the trunk roads is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan.
