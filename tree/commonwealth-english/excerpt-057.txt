Statutory Instruments
Farriers The Farriers’ Qualifications (European Recognition) Regulations 2015
Made18th December 2015
Laid before Parliament22nd December 2015
Coming into force18th January 2016

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 19721 in relation to the recognition of higher-education diplomas, formal qualifications, or experience in the occupation, required for the pursuit of professions or occupations2.
The Secretary of State makes these Regulations under the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

Citation, commencement and extent1.
These Regulations may be cited as the Farriers’ Qualifications (European Recognition) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 18th January 2016.

These Regulations extend to Great Britain.

Amendments to the Farriers (Registration) Act 19752.
The Farriers (Registration) Act 19753 is amended as follows—

in sections 7(1)(f) and (6), 9(3) and 15(1)(d), for “the European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2007” substitute “the European Union (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2015”4;

in section 7(6) and (7)(a), for “regulation 8” substitute “regulation 12”;

in section 7(7), in the opening words, for “regulation 11 or 12” substitute “regulation 15 or 16”;

in section 9(3), for “regulation 9(2)” substitute “regulation 13(2)”;

in section 9(5)— for “regulation 36(2)” substitute “regulation 68(3)”;
