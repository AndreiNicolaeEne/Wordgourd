EXPLANATORY NOTE
(This note is not part of the Order)
The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request from Halite Energy Group Limited under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.

22008 c.29. Paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Planning Act 2008 was amended by paragraph 70 of Schedule 13, and Part 20 of Schedule 25, to the Localism Act 2011 (c.20) and by paragraph 4 of Schedule 8 to the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (c.23). There are other amendments to the Planning Act 2008 which are not relevant to this Order.
