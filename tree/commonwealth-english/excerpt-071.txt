Pensions The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2011

Made Laid before Parliament Coming into force

The Secretary of State, with the approval of the Treasury, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 2 of the Personal Injuries (Emergency Provisions) Act 1939 and now vested in the Secretary of State :

Citation, commencement and interpretation

1 This Order may be cited as the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 11th April 2011.

2 In this Order “the principal Scheme” means the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983 .

Amendments to the principal Scheme 1 For Schedule 3 to the principal Scheme substitute the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to this Order.
