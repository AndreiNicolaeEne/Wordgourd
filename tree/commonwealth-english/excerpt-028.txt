Infrastructure Planning The Infrastructure Planning (Radioactive Waste Geological Disposal Facilities) Order 2015

Made Coming into force The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14(3) and (4) and 232(3) of the Planning Act 2008 , makes the following Order.

A draft of this Order was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 232(6) of that Act.

Citation, commencement and interpretation

1 This Order may be cited as the Infrastructure Planning (Radioactive Waste Geological Disposal Facilities) Order 2015 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

2 In this Order, “ the Act ” means the Planning Act 2008.

Amendments to the Act 1 The Act is amended as follows.

2 In section 14 (nationally significant infrastructure projects: general)—

a after subsection (1)(p), insert— a ; q development relating to a radioactive waste geological disposal facility.

b in subsection (2), for “30” substitute “30A”.

3 After section 30, insert— Radioactive waste geological disposal facilities

1 A radioactive waste geological disposal facility means a facility which meets the conditions in subsection (2).

2 The conditions are that— a the main purpose of the facility is expected to be the final disposal of radioactive waste,

b the part of the facility where radioactive waste is to be disposed of is expected to be constructed at a depth of at least 200 metres beneath the surface of the ground or seabed, and
