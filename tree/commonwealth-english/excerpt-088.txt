Statutory Instruments
Representation Of The People, Wales

The Parliamentary Elections (Welsh Forms) (Amendment) Order 2022
Made22nd December 2022
Laid before Parliament23rd December 2022
Coming into force16th January 2023

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(2) of the Welsh Language Act 19931, as applied by section 22 of the Representation of the People Act 19852.

Citation, commencement, extent and savings1.
This Order may be cited as the Parliamentary Elections (Welsh Forms) (Amendment) Order 2022 and comes into force on 16th January 2023.

This Order extends to England and Wales.

Article 2(2), (3)(a) and (4) does not apply in relation to a parliamentary general election to which paragraph 18 of Schedule 1 to the Elections Act 20223 does not apply.

This Order does not apply in relation to an election in respect of which the date of poll as specified in the notice of election published in accordance with rule 5 of Schedule 1 to the Representation of the People Act 19834 is on or before 3rd May 2023.
