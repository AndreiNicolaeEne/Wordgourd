Weske Wunnoowaonk ne God ayimup nasee wosketomp, yeu anakausuongane Wunnoo- /aonk utttyeu pomantamooonk quoshamomkup Adam, kah ut^ noh wame watonrseongusi ashpe ne teag enniyeuonkpannuppe kah wutinne howaaoowe noswehtatnooonk.

Wosketomp nashpe uppunussiaonk noonunumwaheont wuhhogkuh attumijnunimiate polantampponk nashpe yeu weske anakausuongne Wunnoowaonk, tapeneam God aiyimauoate nahohtpeu Wunnoowaonk^ne n'atiahsiyeue ettamuk Monaneteae Wunnoowaonk;
