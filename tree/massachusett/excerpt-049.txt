Yeu Monaneteae Wunnoowaonk moochekie nompe ut Manittooe wuslukwhonganath hettamuk aynskottumooe Kuttooonk, kah yeu ene nih wacheyeuoo wunnuppooonk Christ Noh aynskottum,kah wacheyeuoo michemohtae Ahtoonk, kah wame teanteaguailinith wachiyeuugkish nish yeuut Aynskottumukkish.

‘Tokonogque yeu Monaneteae Wunnoowaonk moocheke chippe chaubenumuk wacheyeuoo annooteamooongash kah kuhkoowaongash ut_ Naumatue abquompiyeum, kah mahche Christ peyau ut weyausut, qut onch wutch teagweyeuooonk,kah ummenuhkesuonk yeu Wunnoowaonk pasukoooo, kah neaneyeuoo en wame wunnashauanittue, kah wadchanittue natwontamooongash.
