moomoo negonne wosketompae penuthaonk, kah wame onkatogganash Angelsue, kah woske.

sompae matcheseongath ( kah yeu matta webe nashpe ummugkconaittuonk) kah en yeush mat.

cheseongash anue waantamwe kah menuhkesue age Kubkuhbheeg kah onkatogane wanuhunk, kah nanawunau neaunk nehenwonche anué wunhetupanatamwe negonne unnantamooongash;

qut onch matcheseae ennomaiyeuonk yeuth matcheseongath webe oomoounco wutche Oowaasineeg, matta wutch Godut, noh Wwunnhetupanatamweyeuoo —kah lampwiyeuoo, matta kezteauenuoo, asuh wekontamwaenuoo matchesenate.
