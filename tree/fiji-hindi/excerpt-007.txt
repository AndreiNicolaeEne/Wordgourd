Indigenous peoples Vancouver Island me hajaaro saal se rahe hae, Spanish aur British naval expeditions ke late 18th century me aae se bahut din pahile. Spanish aur British saathe hoe ke iske naam Quadra's and Vancouver's Island rakhe rahin ii duuno des ke dosti ke commemorate kar ke jab 1792 Spanish commander of Fort San Miguel, Nootka Sound me, Juan Francisco de la Bodega y Quadra, aur British naval captain George Vancouver, during the Nootka Crisis ke biich me. (Bodega y Quadra's ke naam ke baad me drop kar dewa gais rahaa) Ii North America ke dher jagha hae jiske naam George Vancouver, jon 1791 aur 1794 explored the Pacific Northwest, ke rakkha gais rahaa.
