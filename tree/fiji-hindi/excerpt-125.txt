South Korea East Asia ke Korean peninsula ke ek des hae. Iske capital city Seoul hae. Iske official naam Republic of Korea (Hangul (native Korean script): 대한민국; Hanja (Chinese characters Korean me ): 大韓民國) hae.

South Korea , World War II ke baad suruu bhais jab Japan ke surrender ke baad Korea ke south hissa ke America ke niche kar dewa gais rahaa.

South Korea ke north me ek des North Korea hae, west me hae Piyar Samundar aur east me Japan ke samundar hae. Duuno North Korea aur South Korea apne ke puura Korean Peninsula ke sarkar maane hae. South Korea ke abaadi 51.71 million hae, jisme se aadha log Seoul Metropolitan Area me rahe hae.

Korean Peninsula me Lower Paleolithic period se log rahe hae. Iske pahila kingdom 7th century BC me suruu bhais rahaa. 59 BC me hian pe tiin kingdom, Goguryeo, Baekje aur Silla rahaa, aur 7th century AD talak Goguryeo kindom puura peninsula ke aapan niche kar liis. Goguryeo dynasty (918 - 1392) ke kaaran Korea sab din ek des rahaa. Iake baad Joseon dynasty (1392-1897) rahaa, jiske time Korea me sanskriti, economy aur science me aage barrhaa aur des me santi rahaa. Iske baad ke Korean Empire 1897 se 1910 talak rahaa jab des ke Japan aapan niche kar liis rahaa. Japan hian pe World War II ke ant (1945) talak rahaa jab des ke dui hissa me kar dewa gais. North ke Soviet Union occupy karis aur south ke United States. 1948 me jab unification ke baat tuut gais tab north ek communist des banaa aur south democratic rahaa.

1950 me North Korea, South ke invade karis aur ii larrai jon 1953 talak chalaa me North aur South Korea ke saathe Soviet Union, China aur United States bhi bhaag liis rahaa. Larrai ke ant me koi peace treaty nai rahaa. Ii larrai me tiin million log bar gae rahin. Iske baad South Korea me dher dictator log rahaa, lekin ii jagha ke economy jor se barrhaa Ur iske chaar Asian tiger economy me se ek maana jaat rahaa. 1987 me dictatorship khlaas bhais aur sixth republic banaa.
