1888 me Chile, Easter Island ke aapan niche kar liis. 1966 me Rapa Nui ke log ke Chilean citizenship dewa gais rahaa. 2007, ii island ke "special territory" ke status dewa gais rahaa . Administratively, it Valparaíso Region ke niche hae. 2017 ke Chilean census me island ke abaadi 7,750 log rahaa, jisme se 3,512 (45%) Rapa Nui rahin.

Easter Island dunia ke ek sab se duur waala island hae jisme log rahe hae. Iske sab se nagiich ke jagha jahaan pe log rahe hae, Pitcairn Island hae, jon 2,075 kilometres (1,289 mi) duur hae; sab se nagiich ke town jiske abaadi 500 se jaada hae, Rikitea hae, jin Mangareva island me hae, 2,606 km (1,619 mi) duur; sab se nagiich ke continental point central Chile me hae, 3,512 km (2,182 mi) duur.
