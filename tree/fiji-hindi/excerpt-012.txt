2018 me UNESCO me literacy ke definition me chaapa aur likha gais chij ke bhi include karaa gaiss rahaa.

2019 me OECD likha gais text ke definition me include karis.

UNESCO ke ausaar Afghanistan uu des hae jisme dunia bahr ke des me sab se kamtii literacy hae. 2020 me hian pe 10 million lig parrhe likhe nai sakat rahin. Lekin 2016 se des me kuchh tarakki bhais hae. Jab ke 2016-2017 me literacy rate 34.8% rahaa, UNESCO ii batais hae ki iske baad ii barr ke 43% hoe gais hae aur 15 se 24 saal ke larkan me ii narr ke 65% hoe gais hae.
