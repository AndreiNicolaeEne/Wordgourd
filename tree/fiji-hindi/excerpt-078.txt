. Alexander ke janam aur bachpan ke dher legend hae. Puraana Greek biographer, Plutarch ke anusaar, jab Philip aur Olympias ke saadi consummate hoe waala rahaa, tab Olympias sapna dekhis ki uske womb ke ek bijli (thunderbolt) maaris rahaa jiske kaaran aagi duur talak faelaa, bute se pahile. Saadi ke kuchh din baad, ii bola jaawe hae ki Philip apne ek sapna me dekhis rahaa ki uu aapan aurat ke womb ke ek ser ke chaapa se engrave karis rahaa. Plutarch ii sab sapna ke kuchh interpretation diis rahaa: ki Olympias saadi se pahile pregnant rahii, jiske uske womb ke seal indicate kare hae; nai to Alexander ke pitaji Zeus rahaa. Puurana commentators ispe divided rahin ki ambitious Olympias, Alexander ke divine parentage ke khissa banais rahaa, aus ii bhi bolis rahaa ki uu Alexander ke batais rahaa, nai to uu ii sab suggestion ke or that uu ii suggestion ke dismiss kar diis rahaa ise adharmik bol ke.
