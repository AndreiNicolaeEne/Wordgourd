Buddhism, jiske Buddhadharma aur Dharmavinaya ke naam se bhi jaana jaae hae, ek Indian dharam hae, jiske matlab hae ki ii India me suruu bhais rahaa. , aur ii Gautama Buddha ke sabak me biswas kare hae. Buddha 5th aur 6th century BC me aapan teaching ke India me faelais rahaa. Ii dunia ke 4th sab se barraa dharam hae,, jiske 320 million log follow kare hae, jon dunia ke abaadi ke 4% hae. Ii dharam, eastern Gangetic plain me 5th century BCE me suruu bhais aur dhire-dhire Asia bhar me fael gais. Buddhism ke khaas haanth Asia ke log ke sankriti me rahaa hae. 20th century me ii western dunia me bhi fael gais hae.

Riwaaj (tradition) ke anusaar, Buddha aapan chela log ke batais rahaa ki uu log bhavana ke marg pe chale jisse uu log ke dukh (suffering) ke moksha (liberation) mili. Buddha ii marg ke "biich ke maarg" (middle way) naam diis hae jon sanyas (asceticism) aur sensual indulgence ke biich me hae. Ii sikhae ke ki dukh ke kaaran koi chij se bandhan hae aur meditation aur ahimsa se moksha mile sake hae. Uu chaar mahaan sachhaai (four noble truths), aath mahaan maarg (noble eightfold path), "doctrines of dependent origination", karma aur jinda rahe ke tiin chinh (three marks of existence) ke baare me sikchhaa diis rahaa. Aur chij jon Buddhist log observe kare hae me Triple Gem bhi hae.

Buddhist ke biswaas me dher chij hae, jiske baare me dher bhasa me likha gais hae, jaise Sanskrit, Pali, Tibetan, and Chinese). Dui khaas Buddhist ke vichaar hae jon Buddha ke sikchhaa ke alag se interpret kare hae. , ii hae Theravada aur Mahayana. Theravada me nirvana (jiske matlab hae janam aur maut ke cycle ke khalaas karna) ke emphasise karaa jaae hae aur Mahayana, jisme "liberation of all sentient beings" ke emphasise karaa jaae hae. Iske alaawa, Vajrayana, jisme tantrik technique hae ke Mahayana ke ek hissa maana jaae hae.
