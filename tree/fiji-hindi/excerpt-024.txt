Sukhrgrah (pratiik: ) Saur Mandal (Solar System) me suraj se duur duusra planet hae. Iske dunia ke "twin", nai to "sister" planet bhi bola jaae hae, kaaheki ii size aur mass me lagbhag dunia ke baraabar hae. Jab ki duuno patthar ke banaa hae, Venus ke atmosphere dunia se jaada dense hae. Iske atmosphere me jaada kar ke carbon dioxide hae, isme sulfuric acid ke baadal hae aur koi liquid paani nai hae. Iske surface pe temperature 737 K (464 °C; 867 °F) talak chal de hae aur iske pressure, dunia se 92 dafe jaada hae.
