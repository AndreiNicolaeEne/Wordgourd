Budhgrah (pratiik: ; Mercury) uu planet hae jon Suraj ke sab se nachdik hae aur Saur mandal (Solar system) me sab se chhota hae. Ii planet jab dunia se dekha jaae hae tab ii bahut chatak dekhae hae, lekin jaada kar ke ii suraj ke bahut nachdik rahe hae aur dekhae nai hae.

Ii grah patthar ke banaa hae aur isme khaali kuchh atmosphere hae.Jab ki ii saur mandal ke sab se chhotaa grah hae, iske surface gravity Mars ke gravity se thorraa jaada hae. Budhgrah ke surface of Mercury dunia ke chandarma ke rakam hae, jisme dher cratar hae. Iske sab se barraa crater, Caloris Planitia, ke diameter 1,550 km (960 mi) hae, jon grah ke diameter ke one-third the diameter, (4,880 km or 3,030 mi) ke one-third hae.
