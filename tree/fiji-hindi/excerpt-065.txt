Rome (Italian Roma), Italy ke sab se barraa city aur capital city hai. Ii Tiber Naddi pe hai aur iske population 2.8 million inhabitants log hai aur iske area 1,285 km2 (496.1 sq mi). Vatican City ii city ke north-west hissa me hai. Rome, Europe ke tiisra sab se barraa aabaadi waala city hae. Ii city ke puura metropolitan area me 4,355,725 log rahe hae, jiske matlab hae ki ii Italy ke sab se barraa metropolitan area hae.

Rome ke "City of Seven Hills" bola jaae hae, kaaheki ii jagha me sat pahaarr hae, aur iske "Eternal City" bhi bola jaae hae. Rome western civilization ke suruu hoe waala jagha jaana jaawe hae aur ii Catholic Church ke rajdhani hae.

Rome ke itihaas 2,800 saal puraana hae aur 753 BC me Rome ke suruwaat se suruu hoe hae, lekin hian pe insaan aur pahile se rahe hae aur ii Europe ke sab se puraana city hae jisme sab time log rahat rahin. City ke suruwaat ke abaadi Latins, Etruscans aur Sabines ke mixture rahaa. Baad me ii city Roman Kingdom, Roman Republic aur Roman Empire ke capital rahaa. Iske sab se pahile Roman kavi, Tibullus, 1st century BC me "The Eternal City" bolis rahaa aur baad me iske duusra lekhak bhi kaam me laae rahin.

Roman Empire ke khalaas hoe ke baad, jiske Middle Ages ke suruwaat bhi maana jaae hae, Rome dhire dhire Pope ke influence ke niche hoe gais aur 8th century me ii Papal Staes ke capital banaa, aur ii 1870 talak rahaa. Renaissance ke suruwaat se, Pope Nicholas (1447-1455) ke baad, agle 400 saal talak Rome ke dunia ke artistic aur sankritic centre banae ke kosis karin. Ii rakam se Rome Renaissance ke ek khaas jagha banaa. Kuchh famous artists, painters, sculptures aur architects Rome se aapan kaam karin. 1871 me Rome Kingdom of Italy ke capital banaa, jon 1946 me Talian Republic banaa.
