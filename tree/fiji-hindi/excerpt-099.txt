Turkey, ek republic hai. Turkey me 81 cities hai. Turkish ke paisa Turkish Lira bola jaawe hai.

Iske capital Ankara hai jon ki Anatolia ke biich me hai lekin iske cultural aur economic center Istanbul.

Turkey me 50,000BC se 15,000BC talak insaan aae ke rahe lage rahin. Ii jagha me dher jaat ke log rahat rahin. Alexander the Great ke conquest ke baad, hian pe Greek sanskriti jarr pakarr liis. 11th century me ii jagha me Seljuk Turks aain aur Turkish saskriti hian pe bhi sael gais. 1243 me Mongol log invade karin aur des chgota-chhota raj me vibhajit hoe gais. 1299 me Ottomans des ke unite kar diin aur iske aur barraa karin. 1453 me Istanbul, Ottoman Empire ke niche hoe gais. Baad me ii dunia ke ek sab se taagatwar des ban gais rahaa. 1789 ke baad Ottoman Empire gire ke suruu karis.

The Art of War ek puraana Chinese military treatise hae jon late Spring and Autumn period (roughly 5th century BC) ke hae. Iske puraana Chinese military strategist Sun Tzu ("Master Sun") likhis hae, aur isme 13 chapters hae. Har ek chapter ek alag hunnar (skill) nai to kalaa (art) jon larrai se related hae aur ii duuno military strategy aur chaalbaaji (tactics) ke apply kare hae. Lagbhag 1,500 saal talak, ii sab se important text rahaa aur iske 1080 me Emperor Shenzong of Song iske formalise karis as Seven Military Classics. The Art of War ke abhi talak sab se jaada influence kare waala strategy ke buk hae aur isse duuno East Asian aur Western military theory aur soch pe asar bhais hae.

II book me 5th century BC Chinese military ke puura jaankaari aur chaan-biin (analysis) hae, hatyaar, mausam ke stithi aur strategy se lae ke pad (rank) aur chaal-chalan talak. Sun, larrai me intelligence ke jaruuri ke bhi stress karis hae. Uske, itihaas ke sab se achchhaa sena ke tactician aur strategist maana jaae hae aur uske sikchha aur strategies dunia bhar me advanced military parrhai ke aaadhar hae.

Ii book ke pahila dafe ek Europe ke bhasa me 1772 me anuwaad karaa gais rahaa, jab France ke Jesuit padhri, Jean Joseph Marie Amiot iske French bhasa me anuwaad karis rahaa. Ek British officer, Everard Ferguson Calthrop, 1905 me ise "THe Book of War" ke niche anuwaad kare ke kosis karis rahaa, lekin 1910 me Lionel Giles English anuwaad ke puura karis rahaa. Fauji aur rajnitik neta, jaise Chinese communist krantikari (revolutionary), Mao Zedong, Japanese Takeda Shingen, Vietnamese general Võ Nguyên Giáp, aur American generals Douglas MacArthur aur Norman Schwarzkopf Jr., sab log hae jon ii book se sikghhaa liin hae.
