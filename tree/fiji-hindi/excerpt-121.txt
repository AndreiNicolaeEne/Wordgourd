Moscow (Russian bhasa: Москва, Moskva), Russia ke capital city hae. Iske abaadi 12.5 million hae jiske kaaran ii Europe ke sab se barraa city hae. aur 21.5 million se jaada log iske metropolitan area me rahe hae. Ii city ke area 2,511 square kilometers (970 sq mi) hae, jab urban area 5,891 square kilometers (2,275 sq mi) cover kare hae, and the metropolitan area covers over 26,000 square kilometers (10,000 sq mi). Moscow, dunia ke sab se barraa city me se ek hae. Istanbul, jiske kucch hissa Europe me hae, aur barraa hae aur iske two-thirds population of 15 million Europe me rahe hae, aur European continent ke sab se barraa city hae.
