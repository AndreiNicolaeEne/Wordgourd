2 August 1934 me Hindenburg ke maut ke baad, Hitler head of state aur sarkar ban gais aur uske absolute power dewa gais rahaa. Hitler racist policy ke implement karis jisme uu Jews ke satais. Uske pahila chhe saal ke power me, Germany ke economy jor se barrhaa rahaa. Uu dher jagha jahan pe German log des se baahar rahat rahin ke Germany ke niche kar liis rahaa. Uu des ke sena ke aur taagatwar banais, aur 1 September 1939 me jab uu Poland ke invade karis tab France aur Britain, Germany pe larrai declare kar diin aur World War II suruu hoe gais. 1941 talak Germany Europe ke lagbhag sab des ke aapan niche kar liis. 1941 ke end me America larrau me join kar liis aur 1945 me Germany ke haar hoe gais. 29 April 1945 me Hitler aapan partner, Eva Braun se saadi kar liis aur agle din duuno atm-hatya kar liin.

Hitler ke pitaji, Alois Hitler, Maria Schicklgruber ke ilegitimate larrkaa rahaa. Baptismal register me uske pitaji ke naam nai hae aur suruu me uske surname "Schicklgruber" rahaa. 1842 me Alois ke maiya, Johann Georg Hiedler ke saathe saadi karis. 1876 me Alois ke legitimate kar dewa gais jab uske baptismal record me Johan Georg Hiedler ke uske pitaji likha gais, lekin likha gais naam "Georg Hitler" rahaa. Iske baad Alois aapan surname "Hitler" ke kaam me laae lagaa.

Ek Nazi official ii suggest karis rahaa ki Alois ke maiyaa ek Jewish palwaar me housekeeper rahii aur ii palwaar ke 19 saal ke umar ke larrkaa, Leopold Frankenberger, Alois ke pitaji rahaa, lekin iske baare me koi sabuut nai hae.

Adolf Hitler ke janam 20 April 1889 me Braunau am Inn, Austria-Hungary ke ek toen jon Germany ke border ke nagiich hae, me bhais rahaa. Uu Alois Hitler ke ke chhe larrkan me se chauthaa rahaa aur uu Alois ke tiisra aurat ke larrkaa rahaa. Hitler ke tiin bhaiyaa-bahini janam ke time mar gar rahin. Uske ghar me Alois ke duusra saadi ke larrkan, Alois Jr, aur Angela rahat rahin. Jab Hitler tiin saal ke rahaa tab uske ke palwaar Passau, Germany me jaae ke rahe lagin, lekin 1893 me Alois, kaam ke kaaran, Austria laut aais lekin uske palwaar passau me rahin. Passau me Hitler, Bavarian dialect me baat kare lagaa. 1894 me uske palwaar Austria laut ke Leonding me rahe lagin. 1895 me Alois retire hoe ke Hafeld me rahe lagaa jahaan pe uu madmakkhi paalat rahaa.
