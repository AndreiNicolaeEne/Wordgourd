525 BC me Persia ke Achaemenid Empire, Egypt ke conquer kar liis aur 525 se 402 BC talak ii log ke twenty-seventh dynasty of Egypt rahaa aur ii log bhi pharoah ke title ke kaam me laaae lagin. Fifth century BC me Egyptian log revolt kare ke kosis karin lekin Achaemenids log ke des se nikale nai paae rahin. Alexander the Great Achaemenids ke harae ke Egypt ke aapan niche karis aur Ptolemy ke Governor banais. Isse Egypt me Ptolemaic dynasty suruu bhais.
