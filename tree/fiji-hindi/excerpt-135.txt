September 1875 me graduate hoe ke baad, uu ek artillary regiment me second lieutenant banaa. Uu French mainland me serve karis, lekin 1789 me French Revolution ke baad uu dher time Corsica me rahis jahaan pe uske Corsican nationalism barrhaa. September 1789 me , Napoleon, Corsica laut ke French revolution ke promote karis. Bonaparte ek three-way political struggle, royalists, revolutionaries aur Corsican nationalist ke biich me rahaa. Uu pro-French Corsican Republicans ke join kar ke Corsican independence ke virod karis rahaa. 1792 me Napoleon ke ek Corsican voluteers ke batallion ke commander banaawa gais aur Captain ke promotion milaa.
