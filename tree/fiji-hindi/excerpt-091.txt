Khel ke suruwaat me Ujjar gutti ke pahila rank me ii order me, left se right, rakkhaa jaawe hae: rook, knight, bishop, queen, king, bishop, knight, rook. Pawns ke duusra rank ke sab square me rakkhaa jaawe hae. Black ke gutti ke position, White ke mirror image rahae hae. Satranj ke board ke ii rakam se ghumae ke rakkhaa jaae hae, ki duuno khilaarri ke right-hand corner ek light square rahe hae; iske kaaran white queen sab time light square me rahe hae, jab black queen dark square me rakkhaa jaae hae . Iske ii pharse se yaad karaa jaawe hae "ujjar right me" aur "queen uske rang per".
