Byzantine Samrajya (dosara naam: Pooravi Roman Samrajya), tukarra hone ke baad, Roman Samrajya ke Greek bhaag ke naam rahaa jon ki Middle-ages talak jinda rahaa. Iske capital Constantinople rahaa, jon ki abhi Turkey me hai aur jiske abhi Istanbul ke naam se jaana jaae hae. Jab ki Western Roman Samrajya me Latin bhasa me baat karaa jaat rahaa, Byzantine Empire ke log Greek bhasa me baat karat raha aur iske sankriti Greek rahaa.

Empire ke log, jiske abhi "Byzantines" bola jaae hae, apne ke Romans (in Greek, Ῥωμαῖοι or Romaioi) maanat rahin. Yahii rakam se, uu time ke Islamic log aapan empire ke "land of the Romans" (Bilād al-Rūm) bolat rahin. 800 AD ke baad, Western Europe ke log ii log ke "Greeks" (Graeci) bolat rahin, kaaheki Pope aur medieval German emperors apne ke aslli inheritors of Roman identity maanat rahin. Adjective "Byzantine", Byzantion (Byzantium in Latin) se aais hae, jon uu jagha ke naam hae jahaan pe Greek settlement Constantinople ke banaawa gais rahaa, aur iske khaali city ke log ke describe karaa jaawat rahaa; ii empire ke naam nai rahaa, jiske iske citizens Romanía (Ῥωμανία or "Romanland") bolat rahin.

Jab empire khalaas hoe gais rahaa, tab early modern scholars iske dher naam se bolat rahin, jisme rahaa "Eastern Empire", "Low Empire", "Late Empire", "Empire of the Greeks", "Empire of Constantinople", "Roman Empire". 15th-century historian Laonikos Chalkokondyles, jiske kaam ke Hieronymus Wolf barrhaawa diis rahaa, ke kaaran "Byzantine" aur "Byzantine Empire" ke kaam me laae lagaa rahaa, aur description jaise "Empire of the Greeks" ke saaathe, 19th century talak. Yahii term ke aaj-kal empire ke sab chij khaatir kaam me laawa jaae hae; lekin kuchh historian ii biswas kare hae ki iske nai kaam me laawa jaae ke chaahi kaaheki pahile ii prejudicial aur inaccurate rahaa.
