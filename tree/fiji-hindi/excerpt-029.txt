Ethiopia, jiske sarkari naam Federal Democratic Republic of Ehiopia hae, East Africa ke Horn of Africa ilaaka ke ek des hae. Africa ke sab des me se iske itihass ke baare jaada jaana jaae hae. Aur sab Africa ke des ke rakam Ethiopia koi Europe ke des ke colony nai banaa. Ethiopia ke pahile Abyssinia ke naam se jaana jawat rahaa. "Ethiopia" sabd, Greek sabd Αἰθιοπία se aais hae. 2008 me Ethiopia ke per capita income $1370 rahaa. Ethiopia, Horn of Africa me hae aur iske north me Eritrea, west me Sudan, south me Kenya, east me Somalia aur northeast me Djibouti hae. Iske area 1,100,000 km² (426,400 square miles) aur 2024 me iske abaadi 132,000,000 hae. Iske capital city Addis Ababa hae.
