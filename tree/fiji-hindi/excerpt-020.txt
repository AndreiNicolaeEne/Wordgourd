Portugal, jiske sarkari naam Portuguese Republichae, south-west Europe ke Iberian Peninsula ke ek des hae. Iske west aur south me Atlantic Ocean aur north aur east me Spain hae. Portugal ek chhotaa des hae.

Atlantic Ocean ke tiin island group, Azores Madeira Islands aur Savage Isles Portugal ke hissa hae.

"Portugal" sabd Roman aur Celtic jagha ke naam ke jorr banaawa gais rahaa, aur ii Portus Cale rahaa. Porto sabd Latin se aais hae aur iske matlab "port" hae. "Cale" sabd saait "callaeci" se aais hae, jon ki uu log ke naam rahaa jon north-west Iberia ke occcupy kare rahin. Ek aur theory hae ki "Cale", "port" ke Celtic sabd hae. Ek aur thoery hae ki "Cala" ek Celtic goddess rahii aur kuchh France ke scholars ii biswas kare hae ki ii "Gallus" sabd se aais hae, jiske matlab hae "port of the Gauls".

Lagbhag 200BC me Rome Iberia ke aapan niche kar liis rahaa, second punic war ke baad, aur Cale ke naam ke badal ke Portus Cale (Port of Cale) kar dewa gais aur isme duusra province ke bhi jorraa gais rahaa. Middle Ages me ii jagha ke "Portucale" bola jaae rahaa. 7th aur 8th century e ii badal ke "Portugale" bola jaat rahaa. 11th aur 12th century me "Portugal" naam ke pahile dafe kaam me laawa gais.

Iberian Peninsula ke western hissa, prehistoric jamaa na se log se basaa hae aur sab se puraana insaan ke baskit ke sabuut 5500 BC se hae. Celtic log hian pe 1000 BC me aain. 200BC me ii ilaaka Rome ke niche aais. Iske baad hian pe Germanic log aae ke bas gain. 8th century AD me ii ilaaka Muslim control ke niche rahaa. 1238 aur 1249 ke biich me Christian log hian se Muslim lig ke nikaal diin Portugal, 1139 me ek kingdom banaa.
