Philippines pahunche waala pahila Austronesians , Taiwan se, lagbhag 2200 BC me aae rahin, Batanes Islands (where they built stone fortresses known as ijangs) me aae ke base rahin aur northern Luzon me bhi. Jade artifacts ke 2000 BC ke date karaa gais hae, jisme lingling-o Luzon ke jade ke banaa chij ke Taiwan ke raw material se banaawa gais rahaa. 1000 BC talak, ii islands ke log chaar society me develop hoe gay rahin : hunter-gatherer tribes, warrior societies, highland plutocracies, aur port principalities.

Philippines ke sab se pahila likha gias record, 900 AD ke Laguna Copperplate Inscription hae, jiske Old Malay bhasa me, Kawi script me likha gais hae. 14th century talak, dher barraa-barraa coastal settlements trading centers ban ke societal changes ke kaaran ban gais. Kuchh basti ke log, Asia ke duusra state se chij ke trade karat rahin. Tang dynasty ke time, China se trade suruu bhais, aur Song dynasty ke time aur barrha. Second millennium AD bhar, kuch basti China ke niche rahin. Jaada trade aur diplomacy ke kaaran, Southern Chinese merchants aur migrants Southern Fujian se, aae ke ii islands me rahe lagin, aur ii log natives log ke saathe mix hoe lagin.. Indian ke sankriti, jaise ki bhasa aur dharam ke influence Philippines me 14th century me faela, Indianized Hindu Majapahit Empire se. 15th century talak Islam Sulu Archipelago me aapan adda jamae ke huan se aur bagal faela.

10th aur 16th centuries ke biich me bane waala basti me Maynila, Tondo, Namayan, Pangasinan, Caboloan, Cebu, Butuan, Maguindanao, Lanao, Sulu, aur Ma-i rahaa. Suruu ke basti me tiin -tier ke social structure rahaa: nobility, freemen, aur dependent debtor-bondsmen. Raj kare waala log me neta rahin jaise datus, jon groups (barangays or dulohan) pe raj karat rahin. Jab barangays saathe hoe ke barraa basti banaawat rahin , tab uske neta ke "paramount datu" maana jaawat rahaa, rajah or sultan, aur janata pe raj karat rahaa . 15th aur 16th century ii islands ke population density bahut kamti rahaa jiske kaaran rahaa frequency of typhoons aur Philippines ke location, Pacific Ring of Fire me. Portuguese explorer Ferdinand Magellan 1521 me aais aur ii islands ke Spain ke khaatir claim karis, lekin uske Lapulapu's log Battle of Mactan me maar de rahin.

Sarah Bernhardt, jon ki 22 October 1844 ke Paris, France me paida bhais aur 26 March 1923 me maris, France ke ek stage ke aur iske dunia ke sab se jaada famous actress jaana jaawat rahaa. Bernhardt, Europe ke stage me 1870s me acting suruu karis, aur kuchh din baad me Europe aur United States me kaam kare lagi. Uske ek gambhir actress jaana jaawat rahaa aur uske kuchh log "The Divine Sarah" bhi bolat rahin.

Bernhardt ke janam Paris me Sara-Marie-Henriette Rosine Bernardt ke naam ke niche bhais rahaa. Uu Julie Bernardt (1821, Amsterdam - 1876, Paris) ke, bina baap waala larrki rahi. Julie, Jewish descent ke rahii aur uske jaada kar ke uske suateli maiya, Sara Abraham Kinsbergen, jon ki uske pitaji se 1829 me saadi karis rahaa, paalis rahaa. Julie, Paris me jaae ke rahat rahii.

Bernhardt ke stage ke kaam 1862 me suruu bhais jab uu Comédie-Française, Franceke sab se jaana maana theater, me ek student rahii. Uu France chhorr ke Belgium gais, jahann pe uu Henri, Prince de Ligne, ke mistress banis aur 1864 me ek larrka, Maurice, ke janam diis. Maurice ke janam ke baad, Prince Henri usse saadi kare maaangat rahis, lekin uske plawar iske inkaar kar diis aur Bernhardt ke uske saathe ke ralationship band kare ke majboor karis. Uu baad me ek kafan me sutat rahii.
