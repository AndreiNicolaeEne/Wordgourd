Satranj ke niyam ke FIDE (Fédération Internationale des Échecs; "International Chess Federation"), satranj ke dunia bhar ke governing body, uske Handbook me. Kabh-kabhi jon niyam ke koi des ke governing body publish kare hae, jiske uu sanstha pulish kare hae jon DIDE ke member bai hae, nai to commercial pulishers publish kare hae, FIDE ke niyam se different rahe sake hae.

Satranj ke gutti dher rakam ke rahe hae, lekin Staunton pattern ke sab se jaada kaam me laawa jaawe hae, aur ii competition ke khaatir jaruri rahe hae. Satranj ke sets me dui rang ke pieces rahe hae, jiske jaada kar ke white and black bola jaae hae, aslii rang chaahe koi bhi rahe. Har ek set me 16 gutti duuno rang me rahe hae: ek king, ek queen, dui rooks, dui bishops, dui knights, aur aath pawns.

Khel ke ek square board jisme aath rows (jiske ranka bola jaawe hae) aur aath columns (jiske files bola jaawe hae) rahe hae. Jab ki iiske game pe koi asar nai rahe hae, 64 squares rang me alternate hoe hae aur iske light aur dark squares bola jaawe hae.

Ek Ishwarwaad ((Monotheism) me khaali ek bhagwaan, nai to khaas sevtaa me biswas karaa jaawe hae. Ekmaatr monotheism me khaali ek bhagwaan rahe hae , aur koi devta nai. Pluriform monotheism me dher devtaa rahe hae lekin ii sab ke ek bhagwaan ke dher ruup maana jaae hae.

Monotheism, "henitheism" se different hae, kaaheki isme ek biswas kare waala ek bhagwaan me biswas kare hae lekin ii maane hae ki duusre log jon bhagwaan me biswas kare hae uu ekke chij hae. "Monolatrism" me sher detaa me biswas karaa jaae hae lekin khaali ek ke puja jaae hae. Monolatry sabd ke sab se pahile Julius Wellhausen kaam me laais rahaa.

Ek Ishwarwaad me Atenism, Babism, Bahá'í Faith aur Isai Dharam me biswas karaa jaae hae. Catholic Encyclopoedia, Isai dharam me monothism ke confirm kare hae.

Iron-Age ke samay, South Asia ke Vedic jamaana, me monotheism ke bagal jhukao rahaa. Rigveda me [ Brahman bhagwaan maana jaae hae. Baad ke Hindu biswas dher bhagwaan me biswas hae lekin ek supreme Brahman hae.

China me Shang dynasty (1766 BC) se abhi talak ek bhagwaan me biswas karaa jaae hae.

6th century BC se Zoroastrian log ek bhagwaan, Ahura Mazda, me biswas kare hae.

6th century BC me Judaism me ek bhagwaan me biswas karaa jaat rahaa.

Nile valley ke log 5000 BC me "Waaq", ek aasmaan ke Bhagwaan, ke puja karat rahin. Jab ii log south ke bagal nigrate bhain tab ii logan ii biswas ke rakhe rahin, jaise aaj kal ke Masai, lekin ii biswas ke "ancestor worship" aur spirits me biswas ke saathe puja karaa jaawat rahaa. Dher Nilotic bhasa me baat kare waala log puraana ek Bhagwaan me biswas kare waala log se decendant hae.
