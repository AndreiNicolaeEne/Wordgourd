17 September 1939 me Soviet Union bhi Poland pe larrai suruu kar diis, ii bil ke ki Polish state ab khalaas hoe gais hae. 27 September ke, Warsaw ke bachae waala Poland ke siphai surrender hoe gai. Poland sarkari taur pe surrender bhais aur uske dher siphai duusra des bhaag gain aur baad me Germany ke uppar larrai me saamil hoe gain. Germany aur Soviet union Poland ke ilaaka ke apne me baat liin lekin thora kuch Lithuania aur Slovakia ke de diin. Iske baad Hitler, United Kingdom aur France se shanti ke maang karis , lekin jab iske inkaar kar dewa gais, tab uu aapan army se France pe yudh khatir taeyaari kare ke order karis.
