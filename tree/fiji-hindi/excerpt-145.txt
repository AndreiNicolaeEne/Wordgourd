Britain ke governor, Singapore me 28 January 1819 me aais aur ii island ke ek nawaa port banae ke khaatir achchhaa jagha paais. Ii island pe Tengku Abdul Rahman, Sultan of Johor, raaj karat rahaa, jiske Dutch control karat rahin. Hian ke sultan bahut kamjor rahaa kaaheki uske darbar ke kuchh khaas log sultan ke bhaiyaa ke loyal rahin. British log sultan ke bhiyaa ke raj gaddi pe baithaae diin jiske badle me British pe huan pe ek trading post ke banae ke ijaajat milaa. Treaty of Singapore ke 6 February 1819 me sign karaa gais rahaa.

1824 me, Sultan ke saathe ek aur treaty ke baad, puura island, British Empire ke niche hoe gais. 1826 me, Singapore, Straits Settlements ke ek hissa banaa, jon British India ke niche rahaa. 1836 me Singapore regional capital banaa rahaa. Raffles ke aae se pahile, ii island me khaali lagbhag ek hajaar log rahat rahin, jaada kar ke indigenous Malays aur kuchh f Chinese. 1860 talak hian ke abaadi 80,000 hoe gais rahaa, jisme se aadha se jaada Chinese rahin. Pahile ke immigrants pepper aur gambier plantations me kaam kare ke khaatir aae rahin. 1867 me, Straits Settlements ke British India se alag kar ke , sidhe Britain ke niche kar dewa gais rahaa. Baad me, 1890s me, Malaya aur Singapore me rubber industry suruu bhais rahaa , aur ii island dunia ke rubber sorting aur export ke centre hoe gais rahaa.

First World War (1914–18) ke koi khaas asar Singapore pe nai rahaa, lekin 1915 me British garrison ke Muslim siphaahi log mutiny kar diin rahaa, jab uu logan sochin ki uu logan ke Ottoman Empire ke virod me larre ke bhejaa jaai, jon ek Muslim state rahaa. Ii sipaahi log aapan officer log ke maar diin lekin baad me non-Muslim sipaahi se mutin ke khalaas karaa gais rahaa.
