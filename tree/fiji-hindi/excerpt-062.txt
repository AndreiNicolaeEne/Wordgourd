Adolf Hitler(20 April 1889 – 30 April 1945), ek Austria me paeda bhais German politician rahaa, jon Nazi Germany ke dictator 1933 se, uske maut talak, 1925 talak rahaa. Adolf Hitler, Nazi Party ke neta ban ke, 1933 me Germany ke Chancellor banaa. 1934 me uu Chancellor ke position ke badal ke "Chancellor aur Leader" kar diis. 1934 me Germany ke President, Paul von Hindenburg ke maut ke baat, Hitler, Chancellor aur President ke position ke ek kar kae apne puura Neta hoe gais.

September 1939 me Poland ke invasion ke baad, World War II suruu hoe gais. Uu sab military operation me puura haanth liis rahaa aur uske haanth 6 million Jews ke maut me rahaa.

Hitler ke janam Braunau am Inn, Austria-Hungary me bhais rahaa aur uu 1913 me Germany chalaa gais. World War I me, Hitler German Army me serve karis rahaa aur uske Iron Cross milaa rahaa. 1919 me uu German Workers' Party (DAP), jon Nazi Party se pahile waala party rahaa, ke member banaa aur uske 1921 me Nazi Party ke leader banaawa gais rahaa. 1923 me uu Munich me ek coup kare ke kosis karis aur uske paanch saal ke jail karaa gais rahaa. Lekin uu khaali ek saal talak jail me rahaa, jahaan pe uu aapan autobiography, "Mein Kampf (hamaar larrai) ke likhis rahaa. Uske jail se chhutkaara ke baad uske janata se support mila jab uu Treaty of Versailles ke attack karis, pan-Germanism ke promote karis aur antisemitism, aur anti-communism ke baare me bhaasan diis.

November 1932 talak Nazi Party, Reichstag me sab se barraa party rahaa lekin iske majority nai rahaa. Pahile ke chancellor Franz von Papen aur duusra and other conservative leaders President Paul von Hindenburg pe dabao daalin ki Hitler ke Chansellor banaawa jaae. aur uu 30 January 1933 ke Chancellor banaa. Iske kuchh din Sh Reichstag, Enabling Act of 1933 ke pass karis jisse Weimar Republic Nazi Germany me badle lagaa.
