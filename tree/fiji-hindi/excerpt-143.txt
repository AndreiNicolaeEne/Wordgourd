Easter Island, southeastern Pacific Ocean me Chile ke ek special territory hae aur Oceania me Polynesian Triangle ke souteastmost point hae. Ii island ke iske lagbhag 1,000 extant monumental murti, jiske moai bola jaae hae, jiske Rapa Nui log banae rahin, khaatir jaana jaae hae. 1995 me, UNESCO, Easter Island ke World Heritage Site banais, jisse island ke jaada hissa ke protect karaa gais rahaa.

Experts ii baat pe nai agree kare hae ki ii island ke Polynesian log kaise ii island me aae rahin. Jab ki sher researchers ii biswas kare hae ki ii log 800 ke saal me me aae rahin, lekin 2007 me ek study ii sabuut diis ki ii log 1200 ke nagiich aae rahin. ii island ke log ek thriving aur industrious sankriti ke banae rahin, jiske sabuut hae ii island ke dher barraa-barra, aur patthar ke banaawa gais moai aur duusra chij. Lekin perr ke kaare aurPolynesian rat ke introduction ke kaaran des barbaad hoe lagaa. 1722 me jab European log hian ae ke suruu karin, tab ii island ke abaadi ke 2,000 aur 3,000 ke biich me anumaan lagaawa gais rahaa. European diseases, 1860s me Peruvian slave raiding expeditions, aur duusra island, jaise Tahiti ke emigration ke kaaran island ke abaadi 1877 me khaali 111 rahaa.

Việt Nam, Southeast Asia ke ek des hae. Iske official naam the Socialist Republic of Việt Nam hae. Iske parrosi des hae China, Laos aur Cambodia. Vietnam ke capital Hànội hae aur sab se barraa city Hồ Chí Minh City city hae jiske pahile Sàigòn ke naam se jaana jaawat rahaa. Vietnam me 86.5 million log rahe hae.

Vietnam pe ek dafe Chinese Empire raj karat rahaa tab iske naam Annam ("peaceful south") rahaa.

Aecheologists ke khodaai se ii pataa chale hae ki Vietnam me Paleolithic jamaana me insaan rahat rahin. Kuchh chij jiske khodaai me paawa gais hae ke 780,000 saal puraana rahe ke daawa karaa jaae hae, jiske "tektite ke paae ke kaaran biswas karaa jaae hae, lekin aur log ii bhi daawa karin hae ki ii chij duusra jamaana me bhi paawa jaae hae. 500,000 saal puraana Homo erectus ke haddi ke northern Vietnam ke gufaa me paawa gais hae. Sab se puraana :Homo sapiens" ke haddi ke bhi hian pe paawa gais hae.

Jon jagha abhi Vietnam hae, Maritime Jade Road ke hissa rahaa.

Lagbhag 1000 BC me Ma Naddi aur Red Naddi ke floddplains me dhaan ke kheti suruu bhais aur isse Son sankriti, jiske bronze ke kaam me laae khaatir jaana jaae hae, develop bhais. Ii time me Van Lang aur Au Lac kingdom aur iske influence Southeast Asia ke aur jagha me faelaa rahaa.

Vietnamese log ii biswas kare hae ki Hong Bang dynasty ke Hing raja log 2879 BC me pahila Vietnamese des seuruu kare rahin. 257 BC me aakhri Hung raja ke Thuc Phan harae ke Au Lac ke suruu kare rahin. 179 BC me China ke general, Zhao Tuo, An Duong Vuong ke harae ke au Lac ke Nanyue me jorr diis. Lekon Nanyue 111 BC me Han dynasty ke hissa hoe gais rahaa. Iske baad 1000 saal talak China ke niche rahaa.
