Barents Sea, Arctic Ocean ke ek samundar hae. Ii Norway aur Russia ke northern coast me hae aur ii duuno Russia aur Norway ke territorial water hae. Iske naam, Dutch navigator, William Barentsz se aaia hae. Barents Sea bahut gahiraa nai hae aur iske average gahiraai 230 metres (750 feet) hae aur ii machhrii pakrre ke aur Hydrocarbon exloration ke khaatir jaana jaae hae. Iske south me Kola Peninsula hae, Norwegian Sea west me Svalbard archipekago northwest me, Franz Joseph Land northeast me aur Novaya Zemlya east me. Novaya Zemlya, jon Ural Mountains ke extension hae, Barebts Sea, Kara Sea se separate kare hae.

International Hydrographic Organization, Barents Samundar ke limits ke ii rakam define kare hae:

West me: Norwegian Sea ke northwest limit se ek line jon West Spitzbergen ke southernmost point ke join kare hae Bear Island ke North Cape ke, Cape Bull island ke biich me se aur fir Norway ke North Cape (25°45'E)].

Northwest me: West Spitzbergen ke eastern shore, Hinlopen Strait se 80° latitude north; Nordaustlandet island ke south and east coasts se Cape Leigh Smith talak (80°05′N 28°00′E / 80.083°N 28.000°E).

North me: Cape Leigh Smith se Bolshoy Ostrov Islands (Great Island) Storøya, Gilles, Kvitøya aur Victoria; Cape Mary Harmsworth, jon Alexandra Land ke southwestern extremity me hae fir Franz-Josef Land kenorthern coasts Cape Kohlsaat talak (81°14′N 65°10′E / 81.233°N 65.167°E).

East me: Cape Kohlsaat se Cape Zhelaniya (Desire); Novaya Zemlya ke west aur southwest coast Cape Kussov Noss talak aur fir Dolgaya Bay ke western entrance Cape, (70°15′N 58°25′E / 70.250°N 58.417°E) Vaygach Island me fir Cape Greben; aur fir mainland pe Cape Belyi Noss .
