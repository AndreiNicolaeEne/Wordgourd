Jon roj Alexander paida bhais rahaa, Philip, city of Potidea peninsula of Chalcidice me, ke siege ke taeyaari karat rahaa. Wahii din, Philip ke sandes milaa rahaa ki uske general Parmenion, Illyrian aur Paeonian armies ke ek saathe harae diis rahaa aur uske ghorraa Olympic Games me jiit gais rahaa. Ii bhi bataawa jaawe hae ki ii roj Ephesus me Temple of Artemis, Seven Wonders of the World me se ek, jar ke barbaad hoe gais rahaa. Iske kaaran Hegesias of Magnesia bolis ki ii jar gais rahaa kaaheki Artemis huan nai rahaa, lekin Alexander ke janam me rahaa. Aisan legend saait ii kaaran se suruu bhay rahaa hoi jab Alexander raja rahaa, aur saait wahii iske suruu kare hoi, ii dekhae ke khaatir ki uu superhuman hae aur janam se mashuur hoae ke hae.
