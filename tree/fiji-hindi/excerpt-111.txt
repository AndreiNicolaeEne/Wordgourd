Charlie Chaplin ( 16 April, 1889 – 25 December, 1977) ek jaana maana England ke abhineta aur filam ke banae waala rahaa. Uu suunsaan filam khatir jaana jaawat rahaa, jisme uu kaam karat rahaa, direct karat rahaa, script ikhat rahaa, aur banawat karis.

Charlie Chaplin 70 saal talak kaam karis, paanch saal ke umar se lae ke 80 sal ke umar talak. Charlie Chaplin jaada kar ke "the Tramp" ke character act karis.

Chaplin ke bachpan, London me, gariibi aur musibat ke rahaa. Uske pitaji ghare nai rahaa aur uske maiyaa, paisa ke kami ke kaaran kast me rahii. Iske kaaran Chaplin ke dui dafe, nau saal ke umar se pahile, kaam kare ke khaatir bheja gais rahaa. Jan Chaplin 14 saal ke rahaa tab uske maiyaa ke paagalkhaana me bharti kar lewa gais rahaa. Chaplin chhota umar me perform kare lagaa rahaa, music halls tour kar ke aur baad me stage pe actor aur comedian ke kaam karis rahaa. 19 saal ke umar me, Fred Karno company me sign karis, jon uske United States lae gais. Uske film industry ke khaatir scout karaa gais rahaa aur 1914 se Keystone Studios ke khaatir kaam kare lagaa. Uu suruu me Tramp ke role aapan screen persona ke kaam me laae lagaa. Uu aapan film ke apne direct karis rahaa aur aapan craft ke aur achchhaa karte rahaa jaise uu Essanay Studios me jaae ke kaam kare lagaa, jahaan pe uske Tramp persona emotionally develop bhais rahaa The Tramp (1915) me. Iske baad uske demand aur barraa aur uu act kare ke khaatir aur jaada paisa ke demand karis jab uu Mutual aur First National corporations ke khaatir kaam karis rahaa. 1918 talak uu dunia ke sab se jaada paisa kamae waala aur sab se jaada jaana jaae waala actor rahaa.

Moscow ke baare me pahila dafe 1147 me likha gais rahaa aur ii 15th century me Grand Principality of Moscow ke capital banaa rahaa. Jab 1547 me Tzar log rule kare lagin tab ii political aur economic centre rahaa. Peter the Great ke time capital ke Saint Petersburg me 1712 me kar dewa gais rahaa. 1918 me Russian Revolution ke baad, capital ke ke fir se Moscow ke karaa gaus rahaa. Moscoe Russian Federation ke bhi capital rahaa.

Dunia ke barraa city me se Moscow sab se north me hae aur sab se thandaa hae., jahan pe ii Russia aur eastern Europe ke political, economic, cultural aur scientific centre hae. Moscow ke sab se barra urban economy hae. Moscow me dunia ke duusra sab se jaada billionaire hae. Moscow International Business Center dunia ke sab se barraa financial centre hae aur isme Europe ke sab se uunchaa building hae. 1980 me Summer Olympics, Moscow me bhais rahaa aur 2018 ke FIFA Worls Cup bhi.
