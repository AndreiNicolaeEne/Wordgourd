Har saal Nobel Prizes dher vibhag me dewa jaawe hae. Ii vibhag hae Literature, Science aur Peace. Nobel Prize ke Alfred Nobel ke baad naam dewa gais hae. Iske dunia me sab se barraa prize maana jaawe hae.

Bank of Sweden Prize in Economic Sciences bhi Alfred Nobel ke yaad me dewa jaae hae, aur kabhi kabhi iske Nobel Prize in Economics bola jaawe hae, lekin ii Nobel ke will ke hissa nai hae. Iske 1969 me Bank of Sweden suruu karis rahaa. Ii prize ke aur Nobel Prize ke ssathe dewa jaae hae.

1901 - Emil Adolf von Behring, Germany, ek serum banae ke khaatir jisse log ke diphtheria pakrre se rokaa jaae sake hae

1902 - Ronald Ross, United Kingdom, uske malaria pe kaam kare ke khaatir

1903 - Niels Ryberg Finsen, Denmark, lupus vulgaris (chamrraa waala tuberculosis) ke light radiation se achchhaa kare ke khaatir.

1904 - Ivan Petrovich Pavlov, Russia, uske digestion kaise kaam kare hae ke adhyan ke khaatir.

1905 - Robert Koch, Germany, tuberculosis ke adhyan ke khaatir

1906 - Camillo Golgi, Italy aur Santiago Ramón y Cajal, Spain, nervous system pe adhyan ke khaatir

1907 - Charles Louis Alphonse Laveran, France, uske adhyan protozoa pe, jon ki bemaari ke kaaran bane sake hae, ke khaatir.

1908 - Ilya Ilyich Mechnikov, Russia, aur Paul Ehrlich, Germany, ii paae ke khaatir ki immunity kaise bemaari se larre hae

1909 - Emil Theodor Kocher, Switzerland, thyroid gland pe uske kaam ke khaatir

1910, Albrecht Kossel, Germany, uske proteins aur nucleic substances pe uske kaam ke khaatir

1911 - Allvar Gullstrand, Sweden, light refraction aur aankhi pe uske kaam ke khaatir

1912 - Alexis Carrel, France, uske joining blood vessels aur transplantation of blood vessels aur organs pe kaam ke khaatir

Jane Austen ke janam 16 December 1775 me Steventon, jon ki Basingstoke kenagiichme hae, me bhais rahaa. Uske parrhai uske pitaji aur barraa bhaiyaa logan aur apne se book parr ke bhais rahaa. Uu aapan palwaar ke saathe Steventon me rahat rahii. Jab uske pitaji, 1801 me, retire bhais tab uske palwaar Bath city me rahe lagin. 1805 me, jab uske pitaji ke maut hoe gais tab uske palwwar Chawton, jon ki Alton, Hampshire ke nagiich hae,me rahelagin.

Jane Austen aapan kaam be baare me sehi nai dekhat rahii. Jab uu chhota larrki rahis tab uu khissa likhat rahii. Uske novel ke bahut dafe badle ke baad chhaapa jaawat rahaa. Uske khaali chaar novel ke chhaapa gaisrahaa jab uu jinda rahii. Ii rahaa Sense and Sensibility (1811), Pride and Prejudice (1813), Mansfield Park (1814) aur Emma (1815). Dui aur novels, Northanger Abbey aur Persuasion, ke uske bhaiya 1817 me chhaapis rahaa. Persuasion ke uske maut sekuchhdin pahile likha gais rahaa. Pahile uudui aur book likhis rahaa, Lady Susan, aur ek an unfinished novel, The Watsons. Uu ek nawaa novel, Sanditon, me kaam karat rahii, lekin iske khatam kare se pahile uske maut hoe gais. Uu abhi ek jaana maana lekhak hae.
