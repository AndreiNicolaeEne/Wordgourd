National Basketball Association (Abbreviation NBA) ek American basketball sports league hae jisme 30 teams hae. Ek team me 15 se 20 khiladi hote hae. Lage mein 600 khiladi hote hae. Zyada tar log United States aur Canada ke hote hae aur international kihladi Europe aur South America se bhi shamil hae. Khiladi college aur NCAA se draft ho kar aate hae. Championship Rings di jate hae jitne team ke khiladi(s) ko. Shri David Stern iske longest serving commissioner hae. Iske video games hae; NBA 2k, NBA Live, NBA Playgrounds, NBA Street. NBA ke legends bhi hote hae.
