An diwysyans boos a ell chanjya boos askorrys gen tiogow po lowarthoryon. Boos dyghtys a gomprehend devnydhyow naturel ha keworansow boos pekara kyfeythednow (preservatives), gorthoksidednow, emolsyednow (emulsifiers) ha moggheroryon sawer (flavour enhancers). Rag ensampel, bara ew boos dyghtys.

Dyghtya boos ew gwres tre e’n gegin gen kog. Terweythyow an kog a wra devnydh a lever kegina. Ensamplow a daklow kegina ew pottow, fornow ha padellow.

Pareusi boos a ell bos gwres ewedh en bostiow.
