Drefen bos frutys askorrys dhort oygellow frothushes, nag eus marnas plansow bleujyowa hag a askor frutys. Devisyans esplegyans ew frutys hag a wra gweres keskaryans has gen enevales.

An term losowek a gomprehend lies plans nag ew frutys en styr kebmyn an term, pekara’n losow kegin pompyons, koukomberyow, avalow kerenja, pys, fav, plansow oy ha pubrennow, ha nebes spisys, pekar hag ollspis ha chillis.

Frut gweresek po fug-frothen (pseudokarp) ew frut ma thew radn a’n ‘kig’ derivys dhort matter ogas dhe’n oygel, nag an oygel y honen.
