Ursula Kroeber Le Guin (21 Hedra 1929 – 22 Genver 2018) o awtour Americanek moyha ajwonys rag hy levrow fantasi ha fugieth sciens.

Genys veu Le Guin en Berkeley, Kaliforni, dhe awtour Theodora Kroeber hag anthropologydh Alfred Louis Kroeber. Woja gwaynya mestronieth en Frenkek, Le Guin a dhallathas doktourieth, bes hei a lettyas hebma woja hy temedhyans en 1953 dhe istorior Charles Le Guin. Hei a dhallathas scrifa leun-dermyn e'n 1950ow diwedhes, ow soweni en crytikel ha kenwerthel gen 'A Wizard of Earthsea' (1968) ha 'The Left Hand of Darkness' (1969). Le Guin a waynyas pewasow Hugo ha Nebula rag an eyl, an gensa venen dhe'y wul. Niver a oberoryon settys e'n universow Earthsea po Hainish a sewyas; erel a gomprehendas levrow settys en pow fugiethel Orsinia, niver a oberoryon rag fleghes, ha lies cuntel-lever.

Anthropologieth gonisogethel, Taoieth, feministieth ha srcifow Carl Jung oll a gawas delanwes crev war ober Le Guin. Lies whedhel genjy a usyas anthropolegydh avel an chif corf, ha gwelys ew tybyansow Taoiethel a-dro dhe gespos ha composter en niver a'y scrifow. En venowgh, Le Guin a janjyas tybyansow tipek fugiethel war aga fednow, pecara'n devnydh a dus dewl aga croghen en Earthsea. Themys cowethasek po politek, ow comprehendya hil, reydh, tuedhder ha devedhyans a oos ew apert en hy scrifow. Hei a usyas systemow politek dyffrans en lies whedhel, pecara 'The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas' (1973) ha'n novel direwliethel 'The Dispossessed' (1974).

Scrifow Le Guin o delanwes fest a bris en fugieth sciens ha fantasi. Hei a gawas lies pewas, ow comprehendya eth Hugo, whegh Nebula, ha dew warn ugens Locus, hag en 2003 hei a dheuth an eyl benen enorys avel 'Grand Master of the Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers of America'.
