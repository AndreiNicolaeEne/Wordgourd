| 16 Iken ne Yehovah egh ne s’hakonoronghkwa n’ongwe, nene rodewendeghton nene raonhaon rodewedon rohhawak, nene onghka kiok teyakaweghdaghkon raonhage yaghten a-onghtonde, ok

n'oghwhentjage ongweghne nene enshakodewendeghte n’ongwe, ok nene aontyesenhak raonha raoriwa n'ongwe enshonwadiyadagon.

Raonha nene tehhaweghtaghkon raonhage yaghten enhonwadewenndeght, ok raonha nene yaghten tehaweghtaghkon a onen si ronwadewenndeghton, ne wahhòni tsi yaghten tehaweghtaghkon ne raoghsénagon ne raonha-on rodewedon rohhawak ne Yehovah.
