ne raoronghyakeghro-non ne Royaner yudonghkodatye

Ne o-nenh ne Oges wahhatkaghtho, wahho-neghrako jidehhakanere:

neoni ji-nenthoah wareghde nakwah aakaen-yon, eghwaghshakowennaronke ne Royanerneh nondawe raonhhaken onwe,

neoni ne Niyoh ne Yakweh. Ethoghkeh Noses wahhoyadishonghkwe, neoni yathodennonghyanighden nyaokaen-yonke.

Ethone wahhenron ne Royaner ne raonhhakeh,

wakathonde jiro-nonghshe-nha, neoni dakatsnenghde ne nensekheyadako.

Nihhennyoh kady sathondat, yenkonyadennyeghde Egypt-neh.
