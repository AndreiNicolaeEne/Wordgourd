E Et-hòne Diyògaraskwe neoni Yorheaskwe ne

Neoni Niyoh wahearon, ne Oghwhentsya yawighyarouh konoeheghtsihouh tsinikondiyadòdeanse, Katsènea neoni kondèserese, neoni Karryouh ne Oghwhentsiage tsinikondiyadòdeanse, neoni et-ho neayawea.

f 25 Neoni. Niyoh waghronissa Karhàgouh kònése Karryouh tsinikondiyadòdeanse, neoni Katsénea tsinikondiyadòdeanse, neoni agwègouh kondésere Oghwhentsyàge tsinikondiyadòdeanse, neoni Niyoh wahatkaght-ho, yoyannere.
