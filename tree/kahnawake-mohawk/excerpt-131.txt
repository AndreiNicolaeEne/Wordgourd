. 48 Ethone ne Jews-aka aa apa kanasa wahhonwenhhaghse ne raonhhakeh, Oghnahhoden wadennakeraghdonh waghskwanadonhaghse nohkyonhhakeh, waakwatkaghtho jineken nighsatyerha ne ken-ikenh karighwaokon?

Yesus sahharighwaserako neony wahhenron ne rononhhakeh, Sewanonghsarighsyh ken-ikenh o-nonghsadokenghdy, nok aghsenh ok niweghniserakeh I-ih ensekketsko.
