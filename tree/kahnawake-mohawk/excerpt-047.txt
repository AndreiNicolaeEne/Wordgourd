Shaden-yawenne ji-niyught n’ Akwerenh thaweghdaghkonh ne Niyohneh, neony watkennisaaghdonh ne raonhhakeh ne atdakwarighsyonghsera.

| 7 Ne wahhonny sewaderyendarak onghkaok tyakaweghdaghkonh, ne shakat ne Akwerent ne shakoyenhokonhah.

Neony ne kaghyadonghseradokenghdy, arekho shihjon kando Niyoh thaweronh en-yakorighwakwadakwen enjondadewenniyune ne a adeyakonighriyughntenh ne enkarihhonny ne deweghdagh-konh, yerighwaghnodon raonhhakeh ne Akwerenh arekho ne kaghyadonghseradokenghdy, yakawenh, I-sekeh nendewe aka-Onghwenjakwekonh thiyuderihhokde enghshakoyadaderighste.

Etho kady nen-yawen akaonhha ne ne entyakaweghdaghkonh ne en-yakoyadadery ne shaden-yawenne ne jithaweghdaghkonh n’ Akwerent.

Ikenh ji-niyakonh ne ok yakoyude ne ji-nihhoghtyawenradonh ne na eghyeyadare ne adewendeghtsera:

ikenh kaghyadon naah, En-yondadadewendeghde niyadeyakonh ne ne yaghthayonderighwaghdentyade orighwakwekonh jinahhoden kaghyadon ne kaghyadonghserakonh ne atdakwarighsyonghtsera ne eghnayeyere.

Nok ken-ikenh yahhonghka nonkweh thayakoyadake-nha ne neh ne roderatye ne waghtyawenradonh ne

deweghdaghkonh: nok Ne ronkweh neghnenhhayere ne ky

aghtyawenraghtserakeh, yonkhighsaanyh ne jidewadonnhakaryenh:

ikenh kaghyadon wahhy, Den-yondonnhakary naah niyadeyakonh ne ne ronwayathare ne karondakeh :

Yesus Keristus; ne ne ken-ikenh ne naondon aedewayena ne rorharatstonh kanikonghrakeh aondakayenghdaghkwe ne akarihhonny ne deweghdaghkonh.
