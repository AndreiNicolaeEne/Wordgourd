Ne ronisdenha wa-akawenhaghse ne yondaddenhase tsiok nahhoten enghtsisewenhaghse, eghn'ensewayère.

“negaragbkwata, yaweght tsiniyeweyenòten' ne Jewshaga tsi waondyadohharehha ne ne _toghka

Jesus waghs'hakawenhaghse, sewanaghne ne yeghnegaraghkwathaogon oghneganoghs, ok waenaghne.

Ok waghs'hakawenhaghse, sewaghnegadaghkwen ok eghsewahhawighte tsidhenderon ne ratateristha n'adènnyode, ok egh wa-ehhawighte.
